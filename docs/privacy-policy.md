# Privacy policy

- **App:** Bukin Magnifier (`bukin.magnifier`)
- **Developer:** bukin
- **Contact:** [dev.mechanics998877@gmail.com](mailto:dev.mechanics998877@gmail.com)
- **Effective date:** 2026-11-08

Bukin Magnifier is a magnifying-glass app. It shows the picture from your
phone's rear camera, enlarged, and can turn on the phone's LED light. This
policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

- The camera picture is used only on your phone, to show it on the screen.
- The app has **no internet permission**. Nothing is collected, and nothing is
  sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no tracking.
- A picture is saved only when you tap **Save**, into your phone's Pictures
  folder.
- A picture is shared only when you tap **Share**, and only with the app you
  choose.

## Camera and light

The app asks for the camera permission. It uses the rear camera only while the
app is open on the screen, to show the enlarged picture. The live picture is
processed in the phone's memory and is not recorded or stored. The app does not
record video or sound.

When you tap **Freeze**, the app takes one photo so you can zoom into it. The
photo is kept only in the phone's memory while the app runs. It is not stored
anywhere unless you tap Save or Share.

The light is the camera's LED flash, used as a torch. You turn it on and off.

## Saving pictures

When you tap **Save** on a frozen picture, the app saves it as a JPEG file in
your phone's Pictures folder, in **Pictures/Bukin Magnifier**. The file name
contains the date and time. The file holds only the picture: the app adds no
location or other personal details, and it has no location permission.

Saved pictures are yours. Other apps that can see your photos, such as a
gallery app or a photo backup app you use, can see them too. They stay on your
phone until you delete them, also if you uninstall the app.

On Android 7, 8 and 9, the first time you save, the app asks for the storage
permission, which those Android versions need for saving into Pictures. Android
10 and later need no permission for this.

## Sharing pictures

When you tap **Share**, the app makes a copy of the frozen picture in its own
private storage and opens Android's share sheet. The picture goes only to the
app you choose, which handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. If you
cancel, nothing is sent. The app deletes these copies by itself: a copy older
than 10 minutes is deleted the next time the app opens.

## What the app keeps on your phone

The app remembers three settings: the **zoom**, whether the **light** is on, and
the **colour mode**. It also notes whether it has already asked for the camera
permission. This stays on your phone, in the app's private storage, and is
removed when you uninstall the app.

The app may write technical messages to Android's system log on your phone, for
example a saved file's name and size. They stay on your phone.

## Android backup

If you have turned on your phone's backup to your Google account, or you move
to a new phone with Android's transfer tool, Android may copy the app's three
settings (zoom, light and colour mode). Nothing else from the app is included,
and no pictures. This is done by Android under Google's terms, not by the app.

## Information shared with the developer or third parties

The app has no internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics,
tracking or crash-reporting code. It does not collect, sell or share any
personal information. The developer receives nothing from the app.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google provides the developer with
statistics such as install counts and, if you agreed to share usage and
diagnostics data with Google, crash and performance reports about the app.
Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

If you email the developer, for example from the app's About screen, your email
app sends the message. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and the
developer uses your message only to reply.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included. It is made for
a general audience.
