# Bukin Magnifier

A free magnifying glass for Android: your phone's rear camera, enlarged, with
the LED light. For reading small print, labels, medicine leaflets and price
tags.

## What it does

- **Live magnifier:** the rear camera fills the screen and starts at once.
- **Zoom:** turn the lens ring at the bottom, pinch, or press the volume keys.
- **Light:** the phone's LED torch, on and off with one tap.
- **Freeze:** a sharp photo you can zoom into and move around.
- **Colours:** normal, high contrast, inverted, and yellow on black.
- **Brightness and focus:** make the picture lighter or darker. Focus is
  automatic; tap to lock it, or set it by hand on phones whose camera allows it.
- **Save or share** a frozen picture.
- **Easy to use:** big buttons within reach of one thumb, TalkBack labels on
  every control, English and Hebrew.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer and a rear camera.

## Private by design

No internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no accounts. Nothing leaves your
phone unless you share a picture yourself.
